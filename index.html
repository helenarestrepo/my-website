About me

The bridge between the spreadsheet and the storyboard

I'm an Account Coordinator at GlueIQ, a full-service marketing agency in Miami, where I'm the day-to-day point of contact for six accounts across hospitality, retail and multi-location consumer brands.

My job lives on the management side: timelines, budget pacing, briefs, QA and a lot of client calls. But I'm in the creative process every single day. I review assets, give feedback, and push work from "fine" to "that's the one." I speak fluent client and fluent creative, and I translate between the two so good ideas actually make it out the door.

Born in Houston, made in Miami, and a proud University of Miami 'Cane. 🧡💚

  • 🐶 Dog mom of seven. Yes, seven. Coordinating six accounts is easy after coordinating seven breakfasts.
  • 🎬 Film, TV & book lover. Find me logging and rating everything on Letterboxd.
  • 🍽️ Certified foodie. My Beli rankings of Miami restaurants are taken very seriously.
  • 🎧 Music on, always. Every brief I write has a soundtrack.
