Tobacco Free Florida · Jan – May 2025 · 2026 Gold ADDY Award

Quit Games

An eight-episode reality-style social series that reframed nicotine addiction for Florida teens.

Client

Tobacco Free Florida

My role

Account Executive

Timeline

Jan – May 2025

Channels

YouTube, Instagram, Snapchat

The challenge

Teens scroll right past traditional anti-tobacco ads. Tobacco Free Florida needed a way to talk about nicotine addiction that felt native to the platforms Florida teens actually use and didn't sound like a lecture.

My role

  • Led the strategy, creative and media teams from concept through launch.
  • Conducted six focus groups with Florida teens to shape messaging, tone and platform strategy.
  • Assisted as producer on a five-day production shoot.
  • Ran day-to-day campaign operations.
