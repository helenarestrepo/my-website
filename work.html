process

how i run an account.

⏱️

Budget and scope

Track retainer hours in Productive against a 520-hour monthly cap and flag overage risk early as a scope conversation, not a surprise.

📋

Status and visibility

Weekly client recap emails in a fixed Creative / Website / Social format, backed by an Airtable status base I built and maintain (140 workstreams, 5 tracks).

🤝

Vendors and legal

Coordinate print, signage and production vendors (Miterra, CE Group) and legal counsel on trademark usage, including a ® rollout across a live Webflow site.

⚙️

Process

Recommended and presented a PM tool evaluation (Monday.com vs Asana) to the client, and built the Account Services team's internal AI workflow and model-selection guide.
