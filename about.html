About me

Hi, I'm Helena.

I'm an Account Coordinator at GlueIQ, a full-service marketing agency in Miami, and the day-to-day point of contact for six accounts across hospitality, retail and multi-location consumer brands.

My job lives on the management side: timelines, budget pacing, briefs, QA and a lot of client calls. But I'm part of the creative process every day. I review assets, give feedback, and help push work from "fine" to "that's the one." I speak fluent client and fluent creative, and I translate between the two so good ideas actually make it out the door.
