the challenge

Modernize a multi-location consumer brand and rebuild its website. It's a $200K effort across four disciplines, with a lot of moving parts and a lot of stakeholders.

what i owned

I coordinate the redesign across strategy, UX, creative and development: building and tracking the timeline, running feedback rounds, pacing the budget, and reviewing creative and copy before anything reaches the client.
