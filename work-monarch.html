the challenge

A new luxury hotel had to arrive in the market fully formed: a go-to-market plan, a website, campaign photography, and every printed piece a guest or sales lead would touch, all on one immovable opening date.

After opening, the account became a 6,500-hour annual retainer with brand, digital, social and legal work running in parallel, and a client who needed one person to keep every thread visible and on schedule.

what i owned

launch

  • Grand opening collateral
  • F&B menus and sales materials

retainer (ongoing)

  • Weekly status recaps and Airtable status base
  • Retainer hour tracking against the 520-hour monthly cap
  • Spa membership campaign: trifold, digital brochure, web module, social stories, carousel
  • Website dining and accessibility updates
  • Trademark and ® usage rollout across themonarchsa.com with legal
  • Visit San Antonio co-op Meta campaign
  • 10-second airport baggage-claim F&B spot
  • Wedding, banquet and catering menu production through 11+ versions
  • Vendor coordination (Miterra, CE Group)
  • PM tool recommendation deck
